Privacy Policy – Cursed Defense

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Cursed Defense is a horror-themed lane defense game for Android. This policy explains what data the app touches and why.

Data We Collect

  • Google AdMob — shows interstitial and rewarded-video ads and may collect advertising/device identifiers to do so. Players in the EEA/UK see a consent request (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad-related data is processed.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (Gold, Shards, Warden's Pact) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account or sign-in — the app never asks for your name, email, or any personal information.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.
  • No data sold to third parties.

Game Progress

Your progress, unlocked wards, and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Children

Cursed Defense targets a general audience aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13.
